DIC — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026

DIC is a Chrome extension that explains a word or phrase on a web page in the context of the sentence it appears in. This policy describes what data DIC handles, where it goes, and what the developer of DIC does and does not receive.

Summary

  • DIC has no server of its own. The developer does not receive, store, or see your browsing data, the text you look up, or your API key.
  • When you look up a word, DIC sends a small excerpt of the page text directly from your browser to Google's Gemini API, using an API key that you provide.
  • DIC does not collect analytics, does not show ads, and does not sell or share data with anyone for advertising.

What DIC processes, and when

A lookup happens only when you (a) rest the pointer on an English word for about half a second, (b) select text, or (c) click a follow-up question inside the DIC popup. In Settings you can switch the trigger to "hold Alt/Option" or "selection only".

DIC does not send page addresses (URLs), page titles, whole pages, your browsing history, form inputs, or passwords. DIC does not read text inside input fields or editable areas.

Third parties

Google Gemini API. The excerpt described above is processed by Google under the terms you accepted when you created your API key. Google's handling of that data, including whether content sent through the free tier may be used to improve Google's products, is governed by Google's Gemini API terms and privacy policy. Any usage charges are between you and Google.

Speech. The "read aloud" button uses your browser's built-in speech synthesis. Depending on the voice your browser selects, the browser itself may send the word being spoken to its speech service.

Optional local gateway. Advanced users can route requests through a program running on their own computer (127.0.0.1). In that mode the same excerpt is sent to that local program instead of directly to Google.

DIC shares data with no other party.

Founding-price waitlist (optional)

DIC links to an optional sign-up form hosted on Google Forms. If you choose to fill it in, the developer receives the email address and plan preference you enter. This is used only to contact you about DIC's paid plan. It is not linked to anything you do in the extension, and you can ask for it to be deleted at any time using the contact below.

Limited Use

DIC's use of user data complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. User data is used only to provide DIC's single purpose, explaining text you choose to look up. It is not transferred except as described above, is not used for advertising, is not used to determine creditworthiness, and is not read by any human on the developer's side, because the developer never receives it.

Your choices

  • Remove your API key or change the trigger mode at any time in DIC's Settings.
  • Uninstalling DIC deletes everything it stored on your device.
  • You can revoke or delete your API key at any time in Google AI Studio.

Children

DIC is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If DIC's data handling changes, for example when a paid plan is introduced, this policy will be updated before the change takes effect and the effective date above will change.
